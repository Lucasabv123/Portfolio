02 / ENGINEERING NOTES

Beyond the interface.

A closer look at the decisions
behind the products.

01PERFUMAGICModernizing workflows without losing the rules.＋

The challenge

Move interconnected sales, service, and payment workflows from Google Sheets and Apps Script while preserving the business rules staff rely on.

What I built

A Next.js and TypeScript platform with database-enforced access controls, audit histories, and approval flows. Field staff can report visits from a phone; office staff can review payments and generate commercial PDFs.

The engineering detail

A local Apps Script compatibility runner and regression tests help preserve legacy behavior. Live duplicate-payment warnings link to existing receipts and preserve form data so staff can assess a payment before submitting it.

02PROFERANK ECMaking student feedback easier to find and trust.＋

The challenge

Help students navigate the relationships between universities, professors, and courses while keeping reviews useful and manageable.

What I built

A relational Prisma/PostgreSQL model, Google sign-in through NextAuth, student submissions, review reporting, and an administrator moderation flow.

The engineering detail

School rankings require at least three visible reviews. A separate FastAPI service translates reviews between English and Spanish; translations are cached by language to avoid repeating model inference.
